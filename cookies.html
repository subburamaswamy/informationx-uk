Cookies Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels. This policy covers all of them.

2. What we use

Strictly necessary: none currently. The site does not set cookies to operate.

Preferences (local storage): Where you choose a theme or display option, it is stored in your browser's local storage on your device only. It is not sent to us.

Analytics: we do not currently use analytics cookies. We do not use analytics cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Advertising and tracking: none.

Third-party fonts: Our fonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono) are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, Google receives your IP address and browser details. Google does not set cookies for this. See Google's privacy policy for details. We recommend Option A.

3. Consent

Cookies and similar technologies that are not strictly necessary are only set after you opt in. Because we currently set none, the site shows no cookie banner. If we introduce any, a banner will appear with equal-weight "Accept" and "Reject" choices, and you can change your mind at any time through a "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. See aboutcookies.org for instructions. Blocking some cookies can affect how other sites work.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@informationx.uk.com.
